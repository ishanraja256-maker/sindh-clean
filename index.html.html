Cleanliness campaigns & tips

Recycling tips

  • Separate wet and dry waste at home.
  • Reuse plastic bottles and bags whenever possible.
  • Compost kitchen waste to make natural fertilizer.
  • Never burn plastic — it creates harmful smoke.

School awareness campaign

Organize a "Clean Campus Day" every month. Students can report issues, plant trees, and educate younger students about waste management.
